Demut

German for humility. Our posture before God and one another.

Demut Global is a faith-based youth organization in Kakamega, Kenya, uniting young people into oneness in Christ.

Join us this weekWho we are
One

Young people, one body

We believe a generation that kneels together walks together. Demut Global gathers youths from different schools, villages and backgrounds around the Word, prayer and service, so that no young person in Kakamega faces life alone.

Rooted in Scripture

We study the Bible together and learn to live it out at home, school and work.

Humble in service

We serve our community first, with hands before words.

United across divides

Tribe, church and background do not separate us. Christ joins us.

There is one body and one Spirit.
Ephesians 4:4

What we do

Every programme is built around one aim: helping young people know Christ and stand together.

Youth fellowship

Worship, teaching and open conversation on faith, school and life.

Bible study groups

Small groups that meet weekly to read, ask questions and pray.

Mentorship

Older believers walk alongside young people in character, studies and calling.

Community outreach

Clean-ups, visits and giving projects across Kakamega.
